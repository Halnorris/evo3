Apps, one platform — for residents, trades and property managers.

It's more than repairs.

Evo is more than just repairs — it's knowing how homes and communities work. Behind every reported leak, broken boiler or sticking door is a person trying to get on with their day. We built EVO to make sure that when they ask for help, help actually arrives — and everyone involved, from the resident to the landlord, can see it happening.

Our modern digital approach drives resident engagement and puts helpful, useful information right where people need it: on their phone, in the moment it matters.
