The platform

Property management, made human.

A simple, coherent, end-to-end solution that connects residents, trades, landlords and property managers to support the day-to-day management and maintenance of residential property portfolios. It's the smartest, most intuitive tenant and trades app on the market — because behind every ticket is a person, not just a task.

For residents

Living App

More than a repair reporting tool — Living App is a resident's digital logbook for their home. It's secure storage for tenancy documents, easy-to-find user guides, and property-specific FAQs that help people help themselves, day or night.

  • Report problems
  • 24/7 emergency response
  • In-app messaging
  • Alerts & notifications
  • Easy appointment booking
For tradespeople

Trades App

A simple, effective way for tradespeople to view and accept jobs — and to keep everyone in the loop. Every step of progress on a job is communicated back to both the resident and the landlord, automatically.

  • On-demand job assignment
  • Smart skill & location scheduling
  • Job updates with photos & video
  • Alerts & notifications
  • In-app billing & payments
For property managers

Dashboard

Our digital dashboard empowers landlords and property managers to deliver an exceptional customer experience. Detailed service history is easy to find, and real-time job tracking gives you the insight to drive efficiencies and improve resident satisfaction.

  • Secure online digital dashboard
  • People, places, jobs, events & notes
  • Experienced support team
  • Knowledge base & property logbooks
  • Real-time calendar
